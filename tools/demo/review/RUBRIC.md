# The bar

Two deliverables, scored separately:

- **FILM** — the product film's key frames (1920×1080)
- **STORE** — the Play Store screenshots (1080×1920)

Each is scored on six dimensions, 0–10. **The overall score is the LOWEST
dimension, not the average.** A piece with one broken dimension is broken.

**9.5 requires every dimension at 9 or above AND zero "template tells".**

## Dimensions

### 1. Typography
Type scale discipline (how many distinct sizes — more than three is a fail).
Tracking appropriate to size (display type needs negative tracking). Line
breaks chosen, not accidental. No orphans, no widows, no line ending on a
preposition. Optical alignment, not metric. Consistent leading. Nothing
shrunk to fit.

### 2. Composition
Everything on a stated grid, or deliberately breaking one. Consistent margins
across every frame. Negative space that reads as intentional, not leftover.
No collisions, no near-misses, no element within 20px of an edge unless it
bleeds off decisively. Balance — the frame should not tip.

### 3. Colour and light
A stated palette with a stated rule for where colour is allowed. Contrast
sufficient for the type at playback size. Light direction consistent across
frames. Nothing muddy. If there is a glow or a gradient it must have a
reason.

### 4. Motion (FILM only; score STORE as N/A → 10)
Durations differ by element mass. Entrances and exits differ. Follow-through
and overlap — elements must not all land together. Easing curves chosen per
role. Rhythm varies across beats. **Uniform entrance on four or more elements
is an automatic fail on this dimension.**

### 5. Product legibility
Can a viewer read the actual UI at playback size? For STORE, at Play carousel
thumbnail size. The product must be the subject, not the decoration. A frame
where the design outshines the product fails.

### 6. Distinctiveness
Would a designer mistake this for a template, a Figma community file, or AI
output? Does it look like *this* product and no other? Generic dark gradient
plus large sans-serif plus a floating screenshot is the default AI aesthetic
and scores 4 at best.

## Template tells — any one of these caps the overall score at 7

- More than three distinct type sizes across the set
- Identical entrance animation on ≥4 elements
- A radial glow behind the subject with no light source logic
- A screenshot floating at a tilt with a soft shadow and nothing else
- Type that is centred by default rather than by decision
- Rounded corners at three or more different radii
- A gradient that fades to black at the bottom to make type readable
- Any element placed at a coordinate that is not on the grid

## What a 9.5 looks like

The frame could be published by a design-led company without anyone asking
who made it. Every position is explicable. Nothing is there because it looked
empty. A designer looking for a mistake finds none in sixty seconds.
